ELDRIN TEASER - SUNO NARRATION PROMPT
==================================================

HOW TO USE IN SUNO
- Use "Custom" mode.
- Paste the STYLE text in "Style of Music".
- Paste the EXCLUDE text in "Exclude styles" (if your Suno has this field).
- Paste the LYRICS text in "Lyrics".
- Title: Eldrin - Teaser
- Generate a few versions and keep the one where the voice SPEAKS (does not sing) and the
  ending is close to 1 minute. If it goes longer, no problem: I cut it in the video.
- The lyrics are in Portuguese WITH accents on purpose: without them the voice reads the words wrong.

at the end, 60 seconds


EXCLUDE (paste in "Exclude styles"):
singing, melody vocals, pop, rap, autotune, female vocals, happy
